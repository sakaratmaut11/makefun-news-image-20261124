# THE SOUTHERN LEDGER — Tuesday 24 November 2026

One newspaper front page for a single day: Tuesday 24 November 2026.
THE SOUTHERN LEDGER carries 34 dated stories, every one sourced, spread
across 9 regions on one 1024x1024 page.

The lead stories are the sinking of the Russian warship Merkury,
the Huang case, and the latest from Gaza — each reported as Tuesday's
news, with named sources and dates checked against the day itself.

## What is on the page

- Masthead: THE SOUTHERN LEDGER, dated in full — Tuesday 24 November 2026.
- 34 dated stories across 9 regions, every one sourced.
- Lead trio: Merkury, Huang, and Gaza at the top of the page.
- Region-organised layout so the day's world reads south to north in
  one sweep, with no story carried over from another date.
- A southern-ledger ledger line at the foot of the page tallies the
  day: 34 stories, 9 regions, one date — Tuesday 24 November 2026.

## How it is built

`main.py` is the entrypoint: standard library only, it opens the
`entry.png` beside it, checks the PNG is exactly 1024x1024 and at
most 1 MiB, and writes its bytes exactly to the output.
